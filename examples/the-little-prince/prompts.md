# The Little Prince, visual prompt plan

All six generations use the same original modern literary editorial illustration system: deep cobalt background, clean 2D shapes, age-appropriate Little Prince silhouette, restrained facial detail, ivory structure, warm-gold stars and fox/wheat, and rose red reserved for the rose. No generated speech, subtitles, visible text, logos, photorealistic inserts, or unrelated 3D objects.

## Clip 1, 0–10s

Start close on one red rose growing on a tiny curved planet. Pull back to reveal the Little Prince and then many graphic rose silhouettes in surrounding space. During the final beat, all other roses lose saturation while the original rose remains red. Push into one red petal until it fills the frame.

Narration concept: among so many roses, why is one impossible to forget?

## Clip 2, 10–20s

The red petal resolves back into the rose on the tiny planet. Show the Little Prince watering it, shielding it from wind, and lowering a clean illustrated glass dome. He prepares to leave, walks away, then turns back. A curved ivory reflection on the glass expands into the transition.

Narration concept: he leaves his small planet and the proud, sensitive rose he still cares about.

## Clip 3, 20–30s

The ivory glass reflection becomes the crest of a stylized desert hill. Place the Little Prince on one side and a warm-gold fox far away on the other. He approaches; the fox retreats. He sits. The fox eventually sits too, still separated by large negative space. Finish with the fox tail wiping the frame.

Narration concept: a relationship does not appear instantly; it needs waiting and gradual approach.

## Clip 4, 30–40s

The gold tail wipe reveals repeated meetings in the same place. Show time passing through sun, shadows, and subtle sky changes. With each passage, the Little Prince and fox sit closer. Wheat grows taller and warmer. End with both sitting close as golden wheat sweeps across the camera.

Narration concept: time, familiarity, and expectation slowly make someone special.

## Clip 5, 40–50s

Golden wheat transforms into rose stems and a large rose garden. The Little Prince stands among many red roses. Gradually fade the garden while memories of his own rose appear: watering, wind protection, glass dome. Push into the single saturated rose at the end.

Narration concept: the rose is precious because of the care and time already given to her.

## Clip 6, 50–60s

Pull back from the rose to the Little Prince sitting on his tiny planet. Warm-gold stars appear as memory fragments of the fox, wheat, and glass dome. Continue into a very wide star field. The tiny planet, Little Prince, one red rose point, and a few gold stars remain in large cobalt negative space.

Narration concept: uniqueness often grows after people have spent time accompanying one another.
